01 · DISCOVER

What answers from the internet

Azure public IPs, Defender's internet-facing devices and Entra's app and identity data in. Active mode adds a masscan → nmap → nuclei pipeline to find what is really listening.

02 · ANALYZE

Hold it against Defender

Every reachable address is compared with what Defender for Endpoint is actually watching, so coverage becomes a fact rather than an assumption.

03 · PRIORITIZE

Name the pair nobody else names

Addresses with no sensor that something is already answering on come first. Defender's exposure level and open ports decide the order.

04 · MITIGATE

Onboard, close or accept

Each finding ends in one of three actions: onboard the host, close the port, or record the acceptance. Exports go straight into the ticket.
